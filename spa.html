A Haven of Relaxation

Escape to our serene spa, nestled in the heart of the Warwickshire countryside. In the tranquil Warwickshire countryside, the Spa at Sunday Warwickshire Walton Hall Hotel & Spa offers a peaceful retreat on our historic estate. Located in the contemporary part of the property, our health and leisure club is designed for the modern traveler, providing everything you need to work out and wind down. Enjoy our state-of-the-art fitness studio, indoor pool, sauna, and steam room, all with stunning views of the pretty gardens and grounds outside. Whether you're seeking an active session or a moment of calm, our facilities cater to your every need.

Indulgent Treatments

Pamper yourself with our luxurious face and body treatments, manicures, and pedicures. Our spa features seven beautifully appointed treatment rooms, where our skilled therapists deliver a range of face and body treatments tailored to help you feel your best. From rejuvenating facials to soothing massages, manicures, and pedicures, every experience is designed to restore and uplift. Indulge in a personalized pampering session and let our team create a moment of pure relaxation for you.
