Special Events @ Sunday Walton Hall Hotel & Spa

We understand how important time together is for our guests, so we endeavour to ensure you can relax and enjoy your own special event whilst we look after the details. Our timeless, versatile spaces accommodate 10 to 200 guests, perfect for Christenings, Proms, elegant dinner parties, or celebrations of life.

Sunday Warwickshire Walton Hall Hotel & Spa is set in 65 acres of beautiful landscaped grounds in rural Warwickshire, with the Hall itself dating from the 16th Century. Once the ancestral home of the Mordaunt family, today its secluded setting and extensive facilities make it one of the region’s most versatile and desirable venues for private events.

Flexible Packages

We offer a range of fully inclusive, flexible packages that can be tailored around your individual event requirements. Our packages include everything you would expect as well as:

  • Free standard WiFi for all guests (upgrade available upon request)
  • A greeting from your dedicated event host
  • Your venue fully set up, plus coffee or a range of speciality teas on arrival
  • Inclusive refreshments throughout the event – you tell us when, where, and how often
  • Quality, varied, and healthy dining options – seasonally adjusted and tailored to your requirements
  • LCD screens with user guide and onsite support available for all events
  • Summary ‘one bill’ account covering all charges – with no hidden costs

All of our packages are fully bespoke for your event needs. Simply contact our events team to discuss your requirements.

Our Event Spaces

Directors Suite

Located in the 16th Century Hall, perfect for intimate private dinners or small gatherings with historic charm.

  • Area: 36.66 m² | Dimensions: 7.8m x 4.7m | Height: 4.67m
  • Features: Natural daylight, disabled access

Lawns Tipis

Host unique outdoor events on our manicured lawns, ideal for celebrations with a bohemian flair.

  • Capacity: Up to 200 guests
  • Features: Flexible setup, scenic views

Victorian Gardens

A picturesque setting for garden parties or receptions, surrounded by lush greenery.

  • Capacity: Up to 150 guests
  • Features: Outdoor setting, ideal for receptions

Courtyard Suite

Conservatory Bar
