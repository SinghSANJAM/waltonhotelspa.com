Discover Walton Hall & Hotel

Where timeless history meets modern elegance in the heart of Warwickshire.

Walton Hall

Dating back to the 16th century, Walton Hall’s aristocratic charm is preserved within lush, modern comforts. This Grade II listed, four-star estate, nestled within 65 acres of glorious Warwickshire countryside, overlooks a serene lake. Located near Warwick and Stratford-upon-Avon, just 10 minutes from the M40 and close to Birmingham International Airport, our venue is ideal for both corporate and private events.

Guests can enjoy the restaurant, 240 free on-site parking spaces, a relaxing health & leisure club with pool, and high-speed fibre optic broadband across the estate.

Our Warwickshire Estate

Find Us

Located just 10 minutes from the M40, Walton Hall is easily accessible yet feels a world away.

By Road:
1) From the North (M40 Junction 13): Exit and turn left, then take the first right. Pass through Newbold Pacey and turn left at the T-junction in Wellesbourne. Continue past the Watermill and follow the single-lane road for 1.5 miles.

2)From the South (M40 Junction 15): Exit and take the fourth exit onto the A429 toward Cirencester. Drive through Barford, turn left at the roundabout to Wellesbourne, then turn right after the Watermill and continue for 1.5 miles along the single-lane road.
