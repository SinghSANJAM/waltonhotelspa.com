Accommodation

197 bedrooms blend age-old comfort with modern design with the contemporary Walton Hotel consists of 129 classic double and twins and a further 20 privilege rooms spread over two buildings plus a further 19 more located in The Coach House, just a short walk from reception. There are 11 self service apartments on the grounds and 20 superior rooms and 9 suites located in the 16th century hall itself all boasting beautiful features.
